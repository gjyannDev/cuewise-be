# Behavioral notes for early-career candidates

## Learning and asking for help
Probe how they get unstuck: what they try alone first, how long before they ask, and how they phrase the question. Strong answers name a concrete moment, what they had already ruled out, and what they now do differently. Weak answers say they "just Google it" or never needed help.

## Taking feedback
Ask about a code review or comment that stung. Strong answers describe the feedback, what they changed, and a later habit that came from it. Weak answers stay vague, blame the reviewer, or only list positive feedback.

## Working on a team project
Probe their own part in a school, bootcamp or first-job project: who decided what, how work was split, and one problem they solved themselves. Listen for "I" versus "we"; strong answers make their share clear without dismissing others.
