# Engineering habits

## Testing strategy
Probe what they test and why: which cases get unit tests, what needs an integration test, and what they leave to manual checks. Strong answers tie tests to risk and mention a bug a test caught. Weak answers aim for a coverage number or test only the happy path.

## Code review
Ask what they look for when reviewing and how they phrase a comment that asks for a change. Strong answers separate correctness, readability and style, and explain how they keep reviews small. Weak answers focus on formatting or approve without reading.
