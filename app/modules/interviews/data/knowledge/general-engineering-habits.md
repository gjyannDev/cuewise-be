# Engineering habits

## Testing strategy
Probe what they check before a change ships and why: which cases they cover, how they pick edge cases (boundary values, empty and failure states), and what they leave to a regression checklist or manual check. Strong: ties tests to risk and names a bug a test caught. Weak: tests only the happy path or chases a coverage number. Follow-up: how do you keep a regression suite from growing too slow?

## Code review
Probe how they review a teammate's work, code or a technical solution, and how they phrase a request for change. Strong: separates correctness, risk and readability, keeps reviews small, and explains the why. Weak: comments on formatting or approves without reading. Follow-up: how do you review work in an area you know less than the author?
