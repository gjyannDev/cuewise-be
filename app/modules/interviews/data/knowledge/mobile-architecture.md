# Mobile architecture and releases

## Offline-first and sync
Probe how the app works without a network and what happens when two devices change the same record. Strong answers describe a local store, a sync queue and a conflict rule the product agreed to.

## Releases and crash management
Ask how they ship to the stores: staged rollout, feature flags, crash reporting, and what they do when a release crashes for some users. Strong answers know they cannot roll back an installed app and plan for it.

## Performance and battery
Probe a slow list or a janky animation they fixed, and background work that drained battery. Strong answers profile on a low-end device and name the measurement before and after.
