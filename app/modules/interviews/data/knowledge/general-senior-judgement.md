# Senior judgement

## Ambiguous problems
Probe how they take on a problem nobody has defined, where the cause is not obvious and several teams are involved. Strong: asks what success means and how it will be measured, narrows the problem before proposing a fix, and owns it end to end without waiting to be told. Weak: jumps to a favourite tool. Follow-up: when do you stop investigating and act?

## Technical direction
Probe a technical initiative they drove: the options, how they wrote it down, how they set or changed an engineering standard, and how they brought others along. Strong: includes a reversal plan, a cost they accepted, and juniors they helped carry it. Weak: decided alone. Follow-up: how would you know the direction was wrong?
