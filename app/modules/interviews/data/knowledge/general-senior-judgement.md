# Senior judgement

## Ambiguous problems
Give a vague goal ("make checkout faster") and probe how they would frame it: which questions they ask first, what they would measure, and how they would pick a first step. Strong answers narrow the problem before proposing solutions.

## Technical direction
Probe a decision that affected several teams: options considered, how they wrote it down, how they got agreement, and how they would know it was wrong. Strong answers include a reversal plan and a cost they accepted.
