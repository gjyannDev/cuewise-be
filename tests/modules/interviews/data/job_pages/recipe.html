Lemon Drizzle Cake

This is the cake I bake every time friends come over. It takes ten minutes to mix, forty minutes in the oven, and it keeps for three days in a tin.

Ingredients

  • 225 g softened butter
  • 225 g caster sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 225 g self-raising flour
  • Zest and juice of two lemons
  • 85 g icing sugar for the drizzle

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 degrees and line a loaf tin with baking paper.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then add the eggs one at a time.
  3. Fold in the flour and the lemon zest, pour into the tin and bake for forty minutes.
  4. Mix the lemon juice with the icing sugar and pour it over the warm cake.
