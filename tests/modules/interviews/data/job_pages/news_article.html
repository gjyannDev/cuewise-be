City approves new tram line after years of debate

By a staff reporter, Tuesday

The city council voted on Tuesday to approve a new tram line linking the northern suburbs to the old harbour, ending a debate that has run through three elections. The line will cost an estimated 240 million and is expected to open in four years.

Supporters say the tram will cut commuting times in half for thousands of residents who now rely on crowded buses. Opponents argued the money would be better spent repairing existing roads and bridges, several of which were closed after last winter's floods.

The project manager told reporters that construction would start next spring, beginning with the depot near the river. Shops along the route will receive compensation for lost trade during the works, the council said.

A public meeting on the final route is scheduled for next month at the town hall, and residents can send comments online until the end of the year.
